Proposals — 2026-11-04

4.1 First particle prototype

  1. What it shows: the chart of nuclides as the state of nuclear structure knowledge. Position (N, Z). Elevation/size: count of known levels (ENSDF). Color: years since last evaluation (ENSDF cutoff). Pulse: XUNDL datasets revised this month. Dim marker: nuclides with literature but no evaluation.
  2. Click a nuclide: side panel with its ENSDF levels/half-lives, pending XUNDL datasets, EXFOR entries, recent papers.
  3. Build: R3F + Points + bloom; one committed JSON from nuclide_dict + ensdf_index + xundl_datasets + nsr_nuclides counts; NYSgpt paper tokens for every surface (the design system this sketch ignored).
